# Lantern Reader — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

Lantern Reader is an e-book and web-novel reader with text-to-speech. It was built by an individual developer (GitHub: lalli87) for personal and family use.

## What the app collects
**Lantern Reader has no servers and no user accounts, and it does not collect or sell any personal data.** All of the following stays on your device:

- Books you import, chapters you save from websites, your reading position, bookmarks, highlights, notes and glossary.
- Settings, including any API keys you add (Gemini, Google Cloud, Claude). Keys are stored only in the app's private storage and are never included in backups.
- Audio produced by natural voices, kept in a cache on the device so replays work offline. It can be cleared in Settings.

## Built-in web browser
The Web tab is a normal browser view. Websites you visit can set cookies and see your IP address, as with any browser. When you tap **Save chapter**, the page's text is copied into your library on the device. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

## Third-party services the app may use
These are used **only if you add your own API key** and **only when you use the feature**:
- **Google Gemini** (AI translation, Gemini voices): the text being translated or read aloud is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API. See https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
- **Google Cloud Text-to-Speech** (Google voices): the text being read aloud is sent directly to Google Cloud. See https://cloud.google.com/terms
- **Anthropic Claude** (AI translation, if chosen): the text being translated is sent directly to Anthropic's API. See https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- **Your phone's text-to-speech engine** (Samsung, Google or other): reads text aloud on the device. Some engine voices marked "needs internet" may send text to that engine's provider.

If you don't add any keys, no text leaves your device except the pages you choose to open in the browser.

## Sharing
Data leaves your device only when you choose to share it: exporting a book or highlights, sharing audio files, or saving a backup. You choose where it goes.

## Deleting your data
Delete books and notes inside the app, clear the voice cache in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.
