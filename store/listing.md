# Google Play listing

**App name:** Lantern Reader

**Short description (80 max):**
Save chapters from the web, read offline, and listen with natural voices.

**Full description:**
Lantern Reader turns web chapters and e-books into a calm, offline library you can read or listen to anywhere: in the car over Bluetooth, while relaxing, or as you fall asleep.

SAVE AND IMPORT
• Built-in text-only browser: open a chapter, tap Save chapter, and it becomes the next chapter of a book. Save & next moves on to the following chapter.
• Import PDF, EPUB, TXT, Markdown, HTML, FB2, DOCX and ODT.
• Export any book as EPUB or plain text.

READ YOUR WAY
• Day, sepia, dusk, night (true black) and console themes.
• Your own fonts, size, weight, line spacing, margins and justification.
• Scroll or turn pages, with tap corners for day/night and bookmarks, brightness by swipe, and volume-key page turns.
• Highlights in six colours with notes, bookmarks, and a glossary of words, characters and terms marked in the text.

LISTEN LIKE AN AUDIOBOOK
• Reads aloud with any voice on your phone (Samsung, Google and more) and keeps going with the screen off.
• Optional natural voices: Google Cloud (Chirp 3 HD, Neural2, Studio, WaveNet) and Gemini, using your own API key.
• Lock-screen, headset, Bluetooth and car controls; speed from 0.1× to 4×; sleep timer; carries on into the next chapter.
• Make audio files: MP3 with Google voices, M4A with Gemini or phone voices.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your library stays on your phone.
• API keys are stored only on your device and are never in backups.
